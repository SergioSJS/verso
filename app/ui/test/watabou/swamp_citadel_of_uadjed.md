# Swamp Citadel of Uadjed 

*After being destroyed in a devastating battle a millenium ago the citadel of Uadjed remained abandoned. Recently an undead man-eating wolf has made its lair here. It is said that the citadel is rich with gold and magical artifacts.*

1. A stuffed dwarf with a knife of death wish in their hands.
2. A crate with an arrow-shaped key in it.
3. An arrow-shaped key in a reinforced basket.
4. A lavishly decorated door with two keyholes on the western wall of the hall.
