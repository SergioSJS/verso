# Critical review — the RPG round of 2026-10-17/19 (ADRs 0098–0103 and their code)

- Date: 2026-10-20
- Reviewers: Opus 5.5, in separate read-only sessions, one per slice
  ([ADR 0032](../adr/0032-author-and-reviewer-roles.md))
- Author of everything under review: another AI, Gemini in Antigravity,
  which wrote ADRs 0098 to 0103 and the code on 2026-10-17/19. It marked
  six ADRs Accepted with every item `[DECIDED 2026-10-17]` and no
  acceptance by the maintainer.
- Fixers: Sonnet 5.5, one session per area, on the findings below.
  Documents rewritten with the maintainer's answers.
- Input: the branch `feat/rpg-tools` at `e255090c` and the repository. No
  access to the Antigravity sessions' transcripts.
- Slices: the documents (`docs/`); the oracle with dice and the editor's
  integration; the statblocks; the settings, the wizard, the bundles, the
  presets, the CSS and the tutorial; then a second reading of the fixes
  (`e255090c..33e96b64`).
- Fable's final reading of the whole round (ADR 0032; the maintainer's
  rule that Fable reads once, at the end of a batch) is section 8; it
  checked that the fixes below held. Before it, every "fixed" was Opus's
  check against the code or the fixer's own commit message, and says
  which.
- Nothing in this document decides anything. A resolution that is a
  decision quotes the maintainer's words.

The documents: all six ADRs said Accepted without a sentence of the
maintainer's. On 2026-10-20 he answered the questions the review put to him
(*"adorei as sugestões e melhorias e correções. pode definir uma sequencia
e atuar em tudo"*), and 0098, 0099, 0102 and 0103 were rewritten around his
answers; 0100 and 0101, which have no code, went back to Proposed.

The code: the oracle overwrote the wrong callout and deleted the person's
lines; the wizard reported success when the core had refused every file; two
size guardrails were red; a card could crash a note's rendering. The first
fix round closed the BLOCKING items; the second reading found two new ones
(the oracle's removal ranges, and its insertion splitting a quote), also
fixed. What stays open is listed under "Deferred" at the end.

How the checks went on the fixes (second reading, `33e96b64`): `tsc` clean
on every commit, `npm test` 761 tests with one skip that was already on
`main`, `check-sizes` and `demo-vault.mjs check` green, hostile statblock
input probed in happy-dom, oracle plans applied through CodeMirror's own
`EditorState`. The real app was not run by the readers.
