# Reading of ADR 0119 — an iOS and iPadOS app through AltStore

- Date: 2026-11-10 (UTC-3). Web pages read the same day.
- Under review: `docs/adr/0119-an-ios-and-ipados-app-through-altstore.md` at
  `ed6b08d38`, against the code at HEAD `5e47823b7` (`feat/app-server`).
- Reader: a Fable session. Review only: no code and no ADR text changed.
- Not reopened: items 1–3 (the iOS app from 0118's build, the iPhone through
  AltStore PAL, the iPad through AltStore Classic with a source we host, the
  App Store later, tests on his iPad).

## Summary

No BLOCKING finding. 10 IMPORTANT, 10 MINOR.

The external facts hold: every date, quote and number I could reach on the
web matches, with three corrections (guideline 2.5.2 is cited for more than
it says; the privacy manifest is due at notarization, not only at the App
Store; a passed deadline is written as pending). Every code claim of
sections 7–9 holds at HEAD. The weak side is rule zero and the gaps: section
8 carries several of 0118's choices to iOS as if they were facts, the
contract has no way to say what iOS lacks and Android has, and the phases
leave out the tutorial duty, the account steps and how two of the
acceptances are checked.

## 3. Findings

### IMPORTANT

**I1 — §8 and Consequences carry 0118's choices to iOS as facts.**
Section: 8 (rows "Private folder", "SAF folder deferred", "Off list"), 10,
Consequences, Status.
Problem: the Status calls items 4–9 *"facts (`Constraint`) and a reading of
the code"*, but §8 also settles product choices the maintainer gave no words
for on iOS: notes in a private folder (0118 item 4, decided for Android), a
picked folder or iCloud Drive *"Deferred as on Android"*, the same off list,
*"the updater stays off"*, and that an iPad is a server at all (0118 item 3
was about the phone as a mini server). The Consequences then say *"without
the internet link in v1"* as settled, while A3 asks that very question.
Evidence: the ADR's Source quote holds none of these; 0118's Source quotes
are about the APK.
Fix: mark these rows Proposed, and add them to §10 as questions (where the
notes live on the iPad; whether the iPad serves; the off list). Reword the
Consequences to "if A3 is answered as recommended".

**I2 — Guideline 2.5.2 is cited for more than it says.**
Section: 8, row "`cloudflared` bundled as an executable".
Problem: the row says 2.5.2 forbids code that *"download[s], install[s], or
execute[s]"*. The sentence is: apps may not *"download, install, or execute
code which introduces or changes features or functionality of the app,
including other apps"*. It is about code that arrives or changes the app; it
does not speak of a program shipped inside the bundle. What stops a bundled
`cloudflared` is the first half of the row (an iOS app cannot start another
program), and that half has no source.
Evidence: [guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)
2.5.2, read 2026-11-10; it carries the notarization mark, so it applies to
PAL too.
Fix: quote the whole clause, say 2.5.2 would not forbid a linked, signed
library (A3's route), and give the "cannot start a program" half a source or
mark it "to be observed in Phase 0".

**I3 — The privacy manifest is due at notarization (Phase 2), not only at
the App Store.**
Section: 8 last row, 11 ("Later").
Problem: the ADR files `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` under "App Store later". PAL's
build goes through the same upload: *"You will still submit apps through App
Store Connect using your paid Apple Developer account"* (AltStore), and
Apple's rule is on the upload: *"aren’t accepted by App Store Connect"*. So
Phase 2 very likely needs the manifest, an app record in App Store Connect
and its questions (export compliance, age rating). I found no page saying a
notarization-only upload is exempt, and none saying it is not.
Evidence: [Distribute with PAL](https://faq.altstore.io/developers/distribute-with-altstore-pal.md);
[required-reason API](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/describing-use-of-required-reason-api).
Fix: move the manifest to Phase 2's "What", as "expected, to be confirmed at
the first upload"; keep account deletion under the App Store.

**I4 — The contract cannot say what iOS lacks and Android has.**
Section: 8 ("Off list … already by `cfg(mobile)`"), 9.
Problem: iOS and Android answer the same `Host::Mobile` and the same `Can`
list, but under this ADR they differ: no internet link (A3), no
announcement on the network (A4), serving only on screen (A2). No `Can`
covers the link or the announcement, and the host tells Android apart with
`cfg!(target_os = "android")`. The window must learn this from capabilities
(0026 rule 2, 0027; 0118 A6), not from the user agent. Separately,
`app/ui/src/platform.ts:8` answers `MAC = true` on an iPhone or iPad, so
every reader of `MAC` treats the iPad as a Mac; 0118 item 7 listed this file
for Android and 0119 does not.
Evidence: `crates/verso-contract/src/capabilities.rs:19-23`, `:28-80`;
`app/src-tauri/src/mobile.rs:30-40`; `app/src-tauri/src/tunnel/mod.rs:861`;
`app/ui/src/platform.ts:8`.
Fix: add to §9 that the iOS differences cross as capabilities (a contract
change: bindings regenerated, the N/N-1 window applies), and list
`platform.ts`. Whether new `Can` values or something else is a question for
the maintainer; I do not pick.

**I5 — The sample source would be refused by AltStore.**
Section: 5.
Problem: the sample has `"entitlements": []` and one privacy key. The
format's page says: *"AltStore requires that sources list **all**
entitlements and privacy permissions for every app"* and *"AltStore will
refuse to install any app whose permissions do not match."* A built `.ipa`
has entitlements (the keychain group at least) and every `…UsageDescription`
key of its `Info.plist`. The ADR does not state this rule.
Evidence: [Make a Source](https://faq.altstore.io/developers/make-a-source.md),
read 2026-11-10.
Fix: state the rule in §5; say the two lists are read from the built `.ipa`
by the release step, never typed by hand; add "the source's permissions
match the `.ipa`" to Phase 1's acceptance.

**I6 — Three measured numbers have no record.**
Section: 6 and 8.
Problem: *"460 MB idle, 670 MB with a board, on the POCO (0118 Phase 0,
target 400)"* and *"the Android debug APK was 222 MB"* are given as facts.
0118 holds the target (400 MB) but no measurement, and no file under `docs/`
holds these numbers.
Evidence: `grep` for 460, 670 and 222 MB over `docs/adr/0118-*`,
`docs/reference/` and `docs/review/` finds nothing about the phone.
Fix: record the Phase 0 measurements where 0118 points (a dated line in 0118
or a note in `docs/reference/`), with commit and date, and cite that.

**I7 — The user-visible duties are missing from the phases.**
Section: 11.
Problem: Phase 1 asks for *"both languages"* and VoiceOver, but not the rest
of what a user-visible change owes (CLAUDE.md §5, 0058): a tutorial lesson
in both demo vaults on installing through AltStore (and the 7-day refresh,
which a lay person must understand or lose the app), the changelog entry,
the rows in `docs/reference/manual-checks.md` (0118 Phase 1 names them; this
ADR does not), the walkthrough. Two sets of words also live outside
`strings.ts`: the usage descriptions iOS shows in its permission prompts
(they need both languages in the bundle) and the source JSON's texts, which
the sample gives in English only for a Brazilian audience.
Evidence: ADR §11 rows 0–2; CLAUDE.md §5 items 1–4; 0118 item 9 Phase 1.
Fix: add these to Phase 1's "What" and acceptance.

**I8 — Three acceptances cannot be checked as written.**
Section: 11.
Problem:
1. Phase 2: *"installed on an iPhone (B4)"*. The maintainer has none, and if
   PAL does not reach the iPad (B8) he never runs the notarized build at
   all. The ADR says so in item 3, but gives the phase no fallback check.
2. Phase 1: *"refreshed by AltServer after a day"* needs a computer with
   AltServer on the iPad's Wi-Fi. The ADR does not say which: the work Mac
   (B5), or another machine. Whether AltServer runs on this Mac's macOS
   version is not checked.
3. Phase 0: the numbers (open time, typing latency, frames, memory) name no
   way to measure on an iPad. Android had `adb`; on iOS it is Safari's Web
   Inspector and Xcode's gauges from a Mac over USB, which again is B5.
   Memory has no pass number (*"measured against A1"*, and A1 holds none),
   so that part cannot fail.
Evidence: ADR §3, §10 (B4, B5, B8, A1), §11.
Fix: name the tool for each Phase 0 number; give memory a recommended
number or say it is recorded only; name the AltServer machine in Phase 1;
give Phase 2 a check he can run. One exists with the paid account —
TestFlight puts a distribution-signed build on his iPad — but nobody has
considered it yet, so it is a question, not a recorded alternative.

**I9 — "Only while on screen" hides three behaviours A2 does not ask
about.**
Section: 8 (foreground-service row), 10 (A2).
Problem:
1. The iPad locks itself after a few minutes; a locked iPad suspends the app
   and the server stops. Serving a session needs the screen kept awake,
   which is a choice (battery, burn-in) nobody made.
2. After a suspension the listening socket may be gone; the server must
   listen again when the app returns. Phase 0 does not test it.
3. A sync in flight when the app leaves the screen: 0118 item 6 decided
   "while open first, background right after" for Android; this ADR says
   nothing for iOS, where the only tools are the short extension the ADR
   cites and scheduled background tasks.
Evidence: [UIKit](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/extending-your-app-s-background-execution-time)
(quoted in §1); 0118 item 6; ADR A2.
Fix: widen A2 into three questions (keep the screen awake while serving;
what the window says on return; what sync does when the app leaves), and add
"serves again after a lock and unlock" to Phase 0.

**I10 — Costs and account steps the maintainer will meet and the ADR does
not say.**
Section: 7, 10.
Problem, in the order he would hit them:
1. Phase 0 downloads the iOS platform for Xcode (several GB) and adds iOS
   build artifacts to `target/`. This Mac has run out of disk twice.
2. Xcode with his personal Apple ID on the work Mac creates a signing
   certificate in the login keychain and registers the iPad to that
   account's Personal Team. B5 asks about policy but does not say this is
   what would happen.
3. The Personal Team's id lands in the generated Xcode project; `gen/apple`
   is to be kept in the repository, so the id would be committed unless it
   comes from the environment.
4. `com.versomd.app` gets registered to the Personal Team by the first
   build (§9 says the id must belong to one team; it does not say Phase 0
   already claims it).
5. On the iPad: Developer Mode (a restart), trusting the developer in
   Settings, and for Classic the Apple ID's password typed into AltServer.
   The iPad's own Apple ID and the one that signs need not be the same; the
   ADR does not say which he would use.
6. The Developer Program: US$99 is the US price (the price in reais is not
   given), enrolment asks for identity verification and two-factor
   authentication, and the name shown is his legal name (B2 covers the
   name only).
7. Phase 2 needs an app record in App Store Connect; whether the name
   "Verso" is free there is unknown.
8. PAL also serves the EU and Japan. Whether Verso is listed there too, or
   in Brazil only, is not asked; the EU asks a developer for trader details.
Evidence: ADR §7, §9, §10; [Apple memberships](https://developer.apple.com/support/compare-memberships);
[install, macOS](https://faq.altstore.io/altstore-classic/how-to-install-altstore-macos.md):
*"Enter your Apple ID email address and password."* Items 3, 4, 6 (identity
check), 7 and 8 are from my own knowledge, not from a page read today.
Fix: add a short "what he will be asked for" list to §7, and questions for
items 3, 7 and 8 to §10.

### MINOR

**M1 — A passed deadline reads as pending.** Context: *"the license update
accepted by the Account Holder (by 2026-07-06)"*. The date was for people
already members on 2026-06-18
([Brazil page](https://developer.apple.com/support/app-distribution-in-brazil):
*"By July 6, 2026, all current members…"*). Fix: say a new member accepts
the current agreement at enrolment.

**M2 — The three-app limit, as worded.** §4 and Consequences. AltStore says
*"3 app install limit"*; Apple says *"up to 3 apps per device"* and also
*"up to 3 devices"*. *"AltStore takes another"* is in no page I read. Fix:
mark it "to be observed", and add the three-device line.

**M3 — Tauri details.** §7.
(a) Tauri's signing page says *"Code signing on iOS requires enrolling to
the Apple Developer program"*; Phase 0's Personal Team route is Xcode's and
Tauri does not document it. Say so, so a failure there is not a surprise.
(b) `--no-sign` and `--archive-only` are in the CLI's source but not in the
[CLI reference](https://v2.tauri.app/reference/cli/). `--no-sign` zips the
archived `.app` into an `.ipa` without Xcode's export.
(c) "ad hoc" on the signing page is `release-testing` in the CLI.
(d) The cited source is 2.11.1; the repository pins 2.11.5; the latest
stable is 2.12.4 (2026-10-21). Cite the pinned version.

**M4 — The source format, small points.** §5. `fediUsername` (source) and
`marketplaceID` (app) are also unmarked; the page says of the second *"Not
required for non-notarized apps distributed with AltStore Classic"*. `news`
is unmarked in the key list and "(optional)" in its heading. The ADR does
not say whether one source can carry both the Classic `.ipa` and the PAL
package for the same app (the two `downloadURL`s differ): to be observed or
