# Demo vault sounds — the sample tracks, their source and licence

- Date: 2026-11-01
- Source: two loops by **RandomMind** on OpenGameArt, chosen by the
  coordinator of the 2026-10-31 round for note 36 (*Som e Música na Mesa* /
  *Sound and Music at the Table*), as ADR 0116 item 31 asks ("a short CC0 or
  own-made sample track in each vault's attachments"); they replace the
  synthesised tone of the first build.
- Status: a reference.

## The files

`demo-vault/assets/sons/`, shared by both vaults (the English vault links
`assets/` as the Portuguese one does; the files are embedded once, in
`app/ui/src/demoVault.ts`):

| File | Title | Source | Length | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `taverna.mp3` | “The Old Tower Inn” (loop) | <https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-the-old-tower-inn> | 50 s | 350 420 bytes |
| `estrada.mp3` | “The Bard's Tale” (loop) | <https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-the-bards-tale> | 58 s | 404 723 bytes |

Re-encoded from the author's loop WAVs to mono MP3 at 56 kbit/s.

## Licence

**CC0 1.0** (public-domain dedication), as each page states. Credited in
Settings › Credits (`app/ui/src/credits.ts`, entry `randommind`) and in the
lesson's last paragraph, though CC0 asks for no credit.
