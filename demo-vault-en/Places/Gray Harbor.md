# Gray Harbor

A harbor of dark stone at the end of the North Coast. The fog arrives before noon and only leaves with the night tide; whoever knows the lighthouse comes in without hitting the rocks.

It is where [[Characters/Lia Torres|Lia]] keeps the maps she shows to no one, and where [[Characters/Captain Ruy|Captain Ruy]] sets out from when the group needs a boat.
