# The Lantern Coast

A sample campaign, written for the tutorial: one **world**, one **character**, three nested **Challenges** and the **journal** of one session. The field names are Fractal's; all the text is ours. The note that teaches each step is [[23 - Statblocks]].

## Realism

Closer to **fable than to chronicle**: the sea has a sense of humour, lost things come back to whoever lost them, and death is rare and matters. Violence is dangerous, but it is almost never the best idea.

## Premises

- The coast's lighthouses were lit, generations ago, by a pact with something that lives in the tide.
- A lantern that goes out leaves a stretch of the coast nameless on the map, until someone relights it.
- Whoever lights a lantern keeps a piece of what the light showed.

## World pools

Each Pool has a **Trigger** (what spends it) and a **Consequence** (what happens when it runs out). The world's pool is in the [[Challenge - The Endless Tide|campaign Challenge]].

| Pool | Trigger | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Light | every lantern that goes out | a part of the coast loses its name |
| Memory | every time someone forgets a name | the sea returns something that was not meant to come back |
