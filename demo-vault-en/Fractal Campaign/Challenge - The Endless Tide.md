# The Endless Tide

The **campaign** Challenge. Each Obstacle can be a `[[link]]` to another Challenge: this one points to an **arc**, which points to an **episode**. It is a tree made of notes, and the graph already draws it.

name: The Endless Tide
descricao: The whole coast loses its name, lantern by lantern, and only the right light brings the tide back.
obstaculos:
  - nome: "[[Arc - The Dark Lighthouse]]"
    quebrado: false

    quebrado: false

reserva_1_gatilho: every lantern that goes out
reserva_1_consequencia: a part of the coast loses its name

See the [[World]] and open the arc's **Backlinks** pane to watch the tree grow upward.
